# Client Portal — Recording Script

**Audience:** clients (not staff). This is an onboarding video NEDS shares
with a client after inviting them to the portal — the "SAY" lines below
speak directly to the client, matching `docs/user-guides/client-portal.md`.
**Target length:** ~7 minutes

**Before you record:** log in to a real (or demo) client portal account
that has at least one invoice, one quotation, one active project with a
team member and an update posted, and — if set up — Drishti/SMDost access
and WhatsApp support enabled, so every section has something real to show.

---

## Scene 1 — Intro and logging in (0:00–0:40)
**ON SCREEN:** The portal sign-in page, `https://crm.niranjanenterprises.co.in/portal/login`.
**SAY:** "Hi, welcome to your NEDS client portal! This is where you can see
your invoices and projects, raise support requests, and stay updated on
the work we're doing for you. You'll have received an invitation email
with a link to set your password — click that, choose a password, and
you're in. After that, just come back to this page any time and sign in
with your email and password."

## Scene 2 — Finding your way around (0:40–1:20)
**ON SCREEN:** Point at the left sidebar (or hamburger menu on mobile) —
Dashboard, Services, Quotations, Invoices, Projects, Tickets.
**SAY:** "On the left, you've got five sections: Dashboard for your
account summary, Services for everything we're running for you, Quotations
for proposals we've sent, Invoices, Projects for status and updates, and
Tickets for support. Your name and company are at the bottom — click Sign
out there when you're done."

## Scene 3 — Dashboard (1:20–2:30)
**ON SCREEN:** Portal Dashboard — stat cards, an announcement banner if
one exists, then type a question into "Ask about your account."
**SAY:** "Your Dashboard gives you the summary — open invoices, active
projects, open tickets, right up top. Click any of those cards to jump
straight to that section. If we've posted an announcement — a holiday
notice, a service update — you'll see it as a banner here too. One more
thing worth trying: 'Ask about your account.' Type something like 'When's
my next payment due?' or 'What's the status of my SEO project?' and you'll
get an instant answer based on your own account — it only knows what's
already in your portal, so for anything else, it'll point you to raise a
ticket or contact your account manager."

## Scene 4 — Services (2:30–3:10)
**ON SCREEN:** Open Services → point at Active Services and Retainer
Services sections.
**SAY:** "Services shows everything we're running for your account in one
place — each one with its current status and the name and email of the
person on our team handling it. Click 'View updates' to jump straight to
that project. If you're on a monthly retainer — SEO, GMB, social media —
you'll see that listed too, with the billing frequency and your account
manager. Got a question about a specific service? Use the Raise a ticket
link right there so it's tracked properly."

## Scene 5 — Quotations and Invoices (3:10–4:10)
**ON SCREEN:** Open Quotations → point at status column. Then Invoices →
click Download on one, then scroll to Upcoming Billing Schedule.
**SAY:** "Quotations lists every proposal or estimate we've sent you — the
number, date, status, validity, and total, so you've always got a record
of what was agreed before any invoice goes out. Invoices shows all of
yours with status — Sent, Partially Paid, Paid, or Overdue — the total and
what's still owed, and a Download button for a PDF copy. Click any invoice
number to see the full line-item and payment breakdown. Below the list,
Upcoming Billing Schedule shows your active recurring services and the
date your next invoice will be generated — and you'll also get an email
reminder a week before each billing date."

## Scene 6 — Projects (4:10–5:10)
**ON SCREEN:** Open Projects → click into one → point at Status, Timeline,
Your NEDS Team, and Updates from Our Team. If a Schedule a Meeting link
exists, point at it too.
**SAY:** "Projects shows everything we're building or running for you.
Click into one and you'll see its status, timeline, and Your NEDS Team —
the people handling this specific service, with their email so you can
reach them directly; the Lead is your main point of contact. Updates from
Our Team is where we post progress notes as work moves forward — new ones
show up automatically, you don't need to ask. If you see a 'Schedule a
Meeting' link, click it to open your team member's calendar and pick a
time — it sets up a Google Meet automatically. That link only shows up
once your account manager has it configured."

## Scene 7 — Raising a support ticket (5:10–6:10)
**ON SCREEN:** Tickets → New Ticket → fill subject/description → point at
the "Which service is this about?" dropdown → choose priority → Submit →
open the ticket and reply → point at a "How did we do?" rating on a
resolved ticket.
**SAY:** "Whenever you need anything, go to Tickets, New Ticket — or click
'Raise a ticket' from wherever you are in the portal. Give it a subject and
describe the issue. If you've got more than one active service with us,
you'll see a 'Which service is this about?' dropdown — pick the right one
so it goes straight to the correct person, or leave it as General if
you're not sure. Choose a priority and submit — our team's notified right
away. You can reply anytime to keep the conversation going, right on the
ticket page — and just so you know, any internal notes our team leaves
for each other are never shown to you, you only see what's meant for you.
Once we've marked it Resolved or Closed, you'll see a quick 'How did we
do?' prompt — rating it helps us, but it's entirely optional."

## Scene 8 — Your NEDS Tools and WhatsApp (6:10–6:45)
**ON SCREEN:** Dashboard → "Your NEDS Tools" section, if enabled → click
Open Drishti Dashboard / Open Social Media Dost. Then point at a "Chat on
WhatsApp" button if enabled.
**SAY:** "If you've got access to our other platforms, you'll see a 'Your
NEDS Tools' section on your Dashboard — Drishti for your audit reports and
SEO tracking, or Social Media Dost to review and approve your content.
Click either button and you're signed in automatically, no separate
password. Those links stay signed in for about 10 minutes — if you ever
see a sign-in error, just come back to the portal and click again for a
fresh one. And if WhatsApp support is enabled for your account, you'll see
a Chat on WhatsApp button too — good for anything urgent; it's handled by
the same team and automatically creates a ticket, so nothing gets lost."

## Scene 9 — Security and wrap-up (6:45–7:15)
**ON SCREEN:** Sign-in page → Forgot your password? link.
**SAY:** "A couple of last things. You'll only ever see your own company's
information here — nobody else's. If you forget your password, click
'Forgot your password?' on the sign-in page, enter your email, and follow
the reset link. And if someone else at your company needs access, or you
lose access to your email address, just reach out to your NEDS account
manager and they'll sort it out. That's everything — welcome aboard, and
we're glad to have you."
