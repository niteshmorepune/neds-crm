# Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often from partners. If your
question isn't answered here, contact your NEDS point of contact directly
— we're happy to help.

## 1. Getting access & logging in

**Q: My invitation or password-reset link says it's invalid or expired — what do I do?**
Links are one-time and time-limited for security, so an older link can stop
working once a newer one has been sent. Go to the partner portal login
page and click **Forgot your password?** — enter your email and a fresh
working link will be sent to your inbox right away.

**Q: Is my data visible to other partners, or theirs to me?**
No — you only ever see your own referred clients and your own content
submissions. Nothing is shared across partner accounts.

**Q: Can I share my login with a colleague at my agency?**
We'd recommend against it — each person should have their own login.
Contact your NEDS point of contact to have another person at your agency
added with their own access.

## 2. Referred clients

**Q: How do I know the status of a client I referred?**
Your Dashboard's **Your Referred Clients** section shows each one's
current status — Active, Prospect, or Inactive.

**Q: I referred a client but don't see them on my Dashboard yet — why?**
It takes a moment for NEDS to log the referral on our side before it
appears here. If it's been a few days and you still don't see it, check
in with your NEDS point of contact.

**Q: How do I know what services a client I referred is actually getting, and whether any are paused?**
Click into the client from **Your Referred Clients** to open their account
page. It lists every recurring service (e.g. SEO, GMB, Social Media) and
project with its own status — Active, On Hold, Upcoming, Not Billed, or
Ended — so you can see exactly what's currently running without asking
NEDS directly. Your Dashboard's portfolio summary also shows a total
count of services on hold across all your referred clients.

**Q: How do I see month-by-month what's been collected for a client, and what's still owed between us?**
Open the client from **Your Referred Clients** — their page has a
**Monthly Collections** grid, per service, showing each month as Paid,
Pending, Collected, or Upcoming with the real amount. If anything's
currently unsettled for that client, a **Settlement** card underneath
shows exactly which direction — either "Owed to you by NEDS" or "Your
share owed to NEDS" — and how much. Your Dashboard also totals this
across your whole portfolio at the top.

**Q: What's the difference between "NEDS collects" and "Partner collects"?**
It depends on how that specific client was set up with NEDS. For a "NEDS
collects" client, NEDS invoices the client directly and then owes you a
share each month. For a "Partner collects" client, you invoice the client
yourself outside the CRM — NEDS never invoices them — and you then owe
NEDS a share of what you collected. Either way, it's the same
Settlement card, just showing the opposite direction.

## 3. Content submissions & uploading

**Q: How do I upload files for a content piece?**
On your Dashboard, find the piece under **Your Content Submissions** and
click **Upload files**. Select one or more files (images, video, or PDFs,
up to 50 MB each) and click **Upload**.

**Q: What happens after I upload?**
The NEDS team is notified and reviews your submission. If the piece was
waiting on you, its status automatically moves to **Received** — no
extra step needed on your end.

**Q: I got a link from NEDS that lets me upload without logging in — is that different from my portal login?**
Yes, that's a separate **secure upload link** NEDS can generate for a
specific content piece, valid for 7 days — a quick option when logging
into the full portal isn't necessary. Either way works: uploading through
that link or through **Your Content Submissions** in the portal both move
the piece to **Received**. If a link has expired, just ask NEDS to
generate a fresh one.

## 4. Quotations

**Q: Where do I find quotations NEDS has shared with me?**
On your Dashboard, under **Quotations** — every quotation for a client
you've referred appears there. Click **Download PDF** to get the actual
document — no more waiting for it over WhatsApp or email. A client's own
page also shows a quick Accepted/Sent/Rejected/Draft breakdown of their
quotations, and your Dashboard's portfolio summary totals this across all
your referred clients.

**Q: Can I see quotations for another agency's clients?**
No — same as everything else in the portal, you only ever see quotations
for clients referred by you specifically.

## 5. Earnings & commission

**Q: I don't see "Your Earnings" on my Dashboard — why?**
It only appears if you're on a commission arrangement with NEDS. If you
believe you should be on one, ask your NEDS point of contact to set it
up.

**Q: How is my commission calculated?**
It's a percentage of the pre-tax value of a deal for a client you
referred, credited the moment that deal is marked **Won** by the NEDS
team.

**Q: Why did this month's estimate change from what I saw last week?**
Each month's figures are a **live estimate** until the 1st of the
following month — deals can still be added, edited, or marked Won during
the month, so the number moves with them. Once the month is locked in on
the 1st, it stops changing even if something about a deal is edited
afterward.

**Q: How do I know if I've actually been paid?**
Check the **Paid** status against each past month in your earnings
history. NEDS updates this once a payout is made — the portal itself
doesn't process payments, so this status is the record of what's actually
gone out.

## 6. Security & your account

**Q: I need portal access for someone else at my agency, or lost access to my email — what do I do?**
Contact your NEDS point of contact directly; this can't be self-served
from the login page.
