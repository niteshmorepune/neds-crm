# Partner Portal guide (for partners)

NEDS gives its referral and content-collaborator partners a secure portal to
track referred clients and submit content directly. This guide is for
**partners** — you can share it with them.

## Getting access
1. NEDS sends you an **invitation email** with a link to set your password.
2. Click the link, choose a password, and you're in.
3. After that, log in any time at
   **https://crm.niranjanenterprises.co.in/partner-portal/login** with your
   email and password.

## Your Dashboard
When you log in you'll see two sections:

- **Your Referred Clients** — every client you've referred to NEDS, with
  their current status (Active, Prospect, Inactive).
- **Your Content Submissions** — every content piece you're collaborating
  on with NEDS (title, platform, and which project it belongs to), with its
  current status (e.g. Pending from Agency, Received, Approved, Scheduled,
  Published).

## Uploading content
Next to any content submission, click **Upload files** to reveal a file
picker. Select one or more files (images, videos, or PDFs, up to 50 MB each)
and click **Upload**. The NEDS team is notified and will review your
submission — if the piece was waiting on you, its status automatically
moves to **Received**.

## Security & privacy
- You can only ever see **your own** referred clients and content pieces.
- Use a strong password and don't share your login.
- **Forgot your password?** Click **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in
  page, enter your email, and a reset link will be sent to your inbox.
- If you need portal access, or no longer have access to your email
  address, contact your NEDS point of contact.
