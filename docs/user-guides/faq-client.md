# Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often from clients. If your
question isn't answered here, use **Raise a ticket** or contact your
account manager directly — we're happy to help.

## 1. Logging in & your account

**Q: My invitation or password-reset link says it's invalid or expired — what do I do?**
Links are one-time and time-limited for security, so an older link can stop
working once a newer one has been sent. Just go to the portal login page
and click **Forgot your password?** — enter your email and a fresh working
link will be sent to your inbox right away.

**Q: Can I share my login with a colleague at my company?**
We'd recommend against it — each person should have their own login so
activity and notifications stay personal to them. Contact your account
manager to have another person at your company added with their own
access.

**Q: Is my data visible to other companies, or theirs to me?**
No — you only ever see your own company's invoices, projects, quotations,
and tickets. Nothing is shared across accounts.

## 2. Invoices & payments

**Q: Can I pay an invoice online, and how?**
Yes — open the invoice and click **Pay Now**. It opens a secure Razorpay
payment window where you can pay by card, UPI, netbanking, or wallet. The
invoice updates automatically once payment goes through, and you'll get an
email receipt.

**Q: Can I pay only part of the balance online?**
Online payment currently covers the full outstanding balance in one
payment. For a partial payment, please contact your account manager and
we'll record it for you.

**Q: I paid, but the invoice still shows a balance — what happened?**
If you paid through the portal's **Pay Now** button, it updates
automatically — give it a moment and refresh the page. If you paid another
way (bank transfer, cheque, etc.), it needs to be recorded on our side
first; if it's been more than a day or two, let your account manager know
and we'll check on it.

**Q: When will my next recurring invoice be generated?**
Check the **Upcoming Billing Schedule** table at the bottom of the
Invoices page — it shows the date your next invoice will be raised for
each active service. You'll also get an email reminder a week before each
billing date.

## 3. Quotations

**Q: I accepted or rejected a quotation by mistake — can I change it?**
Once a decision is made it's final in the portal, but it's not final on
our end — just contact your account manager and we'll sort it out with
you directly.

**Q: A quotation I'm expecting isn't showing up — why?**
It only appears here once it's actually been sent to you. If we've told
you it's on its way but you don't see it yet, check back shortly or ask
your account manager.

## 4. Projects & updates

**Q: How do I know what NEDS needs from me to keep a project moving?**
Open the project and check the **What We Need From You** checklist — it
lists exactly what's still pending, and you can upload files directly
against each item.

**Q: How do I get a status update without asking?**
Open the project and check **Updates from Our Team** — new updates from
us appear there automatically, no need to check in. The separate
**Progress Timeline** below it tracks status/milestone changes
specifically (like an item being confirmed received, or the project
moving to Completed).

**Q: How do I schedule or request a call with my team?**
If your project has a **Schedule a Meeting** link, click it to book
directly on your team member's calendar. Every project also has a
**Request a Meeting** option — pick a time and add a note on what you'd
like to discuss, and we'll confirm or send a calendar invite.

## 5. Support tickets

**Q: What's the fastest way to get help?**
**Raise a ticket** from any page in the portal — if you have more than one
active service with us, select which one it's about so it reaches the
right person immediately. If WhatsApp support is enabled on your account,
that works too and is often the fastest option for urgent queries.

**Q: Will I know when NEDS replies to my ticket?**
Yes — check **Notifications** (the bell icon) for an unread count, and
open the ticket directly to see the full conversation.

**Q: Can I see internal notes your team writes about my ticket?**
No — you only ever see messages meant for you. Anything internal to our
team stays internal.

## 6. Notifications

**Q: What shows up in Notifications?**
A new quotation ready for review, a new invoice, a reply on one of your
tickets, or a new update on one of your projects. Click any notification
to jump straight to what it's about.

## 7. Drishti, Social Media Dost & WhatsApp

**Q: I clicked to open Drishti or Social Media Dost and got a sign-in error — what do I do?**
Those links sign you in automatically for 10 minutes at a time. If a link
has gone stale, just return to your portal Dashboard and click it again
for a fresh one.

**Q: I don't see a "Your NEDS Tools" section on my Dashboard — is that a problem?**
Not necessarily — it only appears once your account has been linked to
one of those platforms. If you're expecting access and don't see it,
contact your account manager.
