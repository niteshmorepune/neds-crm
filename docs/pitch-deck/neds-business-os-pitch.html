04 / 15

The diagnosis

One business. Five different systems. None of them talk to each other.

WhatsApp

Where every real conversation actually happens — and where it also disappears.

Excel

A different sheet for leads, for billing, for attendance — never quite the same version twice.

Tally

Keeps the books straight. Knows nothing about the client, the deal, or the promise behind the invoice.

The Notebook

Whoever's desk it's sitting on is the only person who can answer the question.

Memory

The most-used, least reliable system of all. Leaves the building every time someone resigns.

Every one of these is good at exactly one thing. None of them were built to be your business's memory. So the memory becomes you — and that stops scaling the moment you can't personally remember everything.
